## By role

- **Producer or consumer developer:** begin with event meaning and permitted
  inference before schema or handler code.
- **Architect:** add causality, semantic differences, fan-out, replay, and
  multipliers.
- **Operations:** add capacity, load shedding, reconciliation, and stop paths.
- **Security, privacy, or governance:** add declarer authority, provenance,
  retention, purpose, and feedback review.
- **AI or automation builder:** add implementation constraints, tool authority,
  action budgets, loop prevention, and receipts.
