# Participant Consent and Privacy Boundaries

**Packet version:** `events-tools-blinded-v1-2026-08-29`

**Session status:** Not started until the participant accepts below

## What this exercise is

You will use two draft architecture worksheets on a fictional museum scenario.
The session is expected to take about 65 minutes. The purpose is to find where
the worksheets help, confuse, slow, or fail a practitioner. This is not a test
of your job performance or professional competence.

The organizer must separately determine whether any organizational research,
employment, accessibility, legal, or ethics review is required. This document
does not replace those requirements.

## What may be collected

- a random participant code;
- your written responses on the fictional exercise;
- time spent by segment;
- fields you skipped, questioned, or revised;
- neutral observations of how you used the worksheets;
- your retrospective comments; and
- scorer decisions tied only to the coded response.

No audio, video, screen, or keystroke recording is permitted by this packet's
default protocol. A different recording plan requires separate, explicit
consent and a stated retention period before the session starts.

## Do not disclose real information

Use only the fictional facts supplied in the scenario. Do not provide:

- employer, customer, vendor, or colleague names;
- real product, repository, account, tenant, system, or architecture details;
- source code, prompts, logs, traces, configurations, credentials, or secrets;
- production failures, security findings, legal matters, contracts, or metrics;
- personal data about another person; or
- anything you are not permitted to share publicly.

You may say, “I would normally ask for more information,” without giving a real
example. If you begin disclosing protected information, the facilitator will
stop that discussion and may quarantine the affected artifact for deletion.

## Voluntary participation and withdrawal

Participation is voluntary. You may skip a question, request a break, or stop
at any time without supplying a reason. Withdrawal stops new collection. Ask
the organizer before starting how already-created artifacts will be deleted or
retained and whether deletion has any practical limit.

## Accessibility and questions

You may request an accessible format, more reading time, or another reasonable
adjustment. Adjustments will be recorded as protocol context, not scored as a
defect or participant failure.
