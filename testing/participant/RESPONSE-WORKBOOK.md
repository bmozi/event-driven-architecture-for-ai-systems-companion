## 5. Retrospective

Answer from your experience in this exercise. There are no preferred answers.

1. Which field or instruction helped you most?
2. Which field or instruction was hardest to understand?
3. Where did you answer with technical implementation detail because the
   requested decision was unclear?
4. Which field, if any, changed your decision?
5. What did you want to write that had no obvious place?
6. Which repeated or overlapping fields should be revised?
7. What would make either tool unsafe to use without a facilitator?
8. Would you use either tool again on another fictional exercise? Why or why not?
