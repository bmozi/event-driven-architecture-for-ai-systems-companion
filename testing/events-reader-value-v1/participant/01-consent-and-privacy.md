scenario. We are testing whether the materials are understandable and useful,
not testing your job performance, intelligence, or employer.

Participation is voluntary. You may skip a question or stop at any time. The
result is not a certification, employment assessment, security review, or
production approval.

Use only the fictional Pine Hollow scenario. Do not enter names, employers,
customers, real architecture, confidential incidents, credentials, personal
data, or identifiable security weaknesses. The facilitator will stop and
redirect if such information enters the session.
