# Event Meaning and Consequence Workshop

**Duration:** 75 minutes
**Status:** Prepared and unrun
**Outcome:** One event decision with a defensible fact, allowed reactions,
multiplier boundary, owners, and evidence plan

## Participants

Invite a business declarer, technical publisher, representative consumer,
operations/reliability owner, security/privacy reviewer, and facilitator.

## Agenda

### 0–10 minutes: name the value

- Who needs to notice what change?
- What becomes possible if the notice is timely and trustworthy?
- What current polling, delay, manual coordination, or missed intervention is
  visible?

### 10–25 minutes: define the fact

Use the [Meaning-and-Authority Record](event-meaning-and-authority-record.md).
Separate occurrence, declaration, recording, publication, observation, and
notification.

### 25–40 minutes: define reaction and ownership

- What may each consumer infer?
- Which reaction only requests an API capability?
- Which open promise belongs in a workflow?
- Which data meaning or agent authority requires a separate owner?

### 40–55 minutes: expose multiplication

Use the [Multiplier Calculator](traffic-cost-action-multiplier-calculator.md)
and [Failure Lab](FAILURE-LAB.md). Include fan-out, retries, calls, action
attempts, effects, recovery traffic, and feedback edges.

### 55–68 minutes: define evidence and stop paths

- What can be shed, delayed, replayed, or corrected?
- Who reconciles business outcome?
- Which result forces throttling, isolation, redesign, or stop?

### 68–75 minutes: decide

Choose `EXPLORE`, `PROCEED BOUNDED`, `INVEST`, `HOLD`, or `STOP`. Record owners
and review date.

## Required outputs

- meaning-and-authority record;
- permitted and prohibited inference;
- first-order multiplier with unknowns;
- failure-lab detection record;
- value-and-evidence ledger; and
- next decision owner and date.

Stop when the group cannot identify the fact, declarer, permitted inference,
owner of a reaction, or reconciliation evidence. The stop is a useful result.
