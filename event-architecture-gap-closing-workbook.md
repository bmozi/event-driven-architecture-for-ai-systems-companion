## ELI5 handoff

Explain the completed record to someone who does not know your broker or
framework:

1. What happened?
2. Who was allowed to say so?
3. What may a consumer do?
4. What happens if the claim is late, repeated, replayed, forged, or generated
   by an AI participant?
5. What evidence would make us stop?

If the explanation depends on a topic name or product feature, the decision is
not yet portable.
