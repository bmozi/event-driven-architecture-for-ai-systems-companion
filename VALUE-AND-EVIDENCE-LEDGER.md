## Evidence labels

Mark every number `MEASURED`, `REPORTED`, `ESTIMATED`, `SCENARIO`, or `UNKNOWN`.
Keep message activity separate from externally reconciled business outcome.

## Outside-team test

Ask a consumer, operations representative, and decision owner to explain the
value and the stop condition without seeing the producer's implementation.
